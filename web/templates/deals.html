Find an active listing to evaluateAdd an opportunity found elsewhere

Start from Evaluate deal to open Deal analysis, then attach a sale you have verified through the existing comparable-evidence form. It records:

  • sold price and currency
  • sold date, when known
  • condition
  • reference ID and title
  • HTTPS source URL
  • source identity, provenance, and notes

Manually attached sold comparables can satisfy Deal Score's sold-evidence requirement when the remaining economics are complete. They do not change deal-score-v1. An ended listing is not evidence of a sale.
